# Unit 5: Unit 5: Project Management, Quality, Maintenance

## Golden Points
1. Basic COCOMO: Effort E = a times KLOC to the power b person months, Time D = c times E to the power d months, People = E divided by D
2. Intermediate COCOMO multiplies effort by EAF, the product of all cost driver values
3. Function Point FP = UFP times (0.65 + 0.01 times sum of Fi), UFP from EI, EO, EQ, ILF, EIF with weights
4. Critical path is the longest path in the activity network. Activities on it have zero float
5. SCM controls change: baseline, version control, change control, configuration audit, status report
6. Earned value: SV = EV minus PV, CV = EV minus AC, SPI = EV by PV, CPI = EV by AC
7. Risk exposure RE = probability times loss. Risks are known, predictable or unpredictable. RMMM = mitigation, monitoring, management
8. McCall has 11 factors in 3 groups: operation, revision, transition
9. CMM levels: Initial, Repeatable, Defined, Managed, Optimizing
10. Reliability: MTBF = MTTF + MTTR, availability = MTTF by MTBF. Defect density = defects per KLOC
11. Maintenance types: corrective, adaptive, perfective, preventive

## Exam answers / numericals (as shown in video)

### Scene 2: COCOMO: E = a (KLOC)^b, D = c E^d, People = E / D
    ## Formulas
    E = a x (KLOC)^b   person-months
    D = c x E^d   months
    People = E / D
    Intermediate: E = a x (KLOC)^b x EAF
    ! EAF = product of all cost driver multipliers
    ~ Intermediate a values: 3.2, 3.0, 2.8

### Scene 3: Effort = 2.8 x 50^1.2 x 0.891 = 272.8 person months
    ## Step 1: EAF
    EAF = 0.75 x 1.10 x 1.20 x 0.90
    ! EAF = 0.891
    ## Step 2: nominal effort
    50^1.2 = 109.34
    E nominal = 2.8 x 109.34 = 306.14
    ## Step 3: adjusted effort
    E = 306.14 x 0.891
    ! E = 272.8 person-months
    ## Step 4: time and people (c=2.5, d=0.32)
    D = 2.5 x 272.8^0.32 = 15.0 months
    People = 272.8 / 15.0 = 18.1
    ! About 18 people for 15 months

### Scene 4: FP = UFP x (0.65 + 0.01 x sum Fi)
    ## UFP
    40 + 40 + 24 + 40 + 14
    ! UFP = 158
    ## VAF
    sum Fi = 42 (14 factors, avg 3)
    VAF = 0.65 + 0.01 x 42 = 1.07
    ## FP
    FP = 158 x 1.07
    ! FP = 169

### Scene 5: Critical path = longest path, zero float
    ## Forward pass (ES - EF)
    A 0-3, B 3-7, C 3-5
    D 7-12, E 5-8, F 12-14
    ## Paths
    A-B-D-F = 3+4+5+2 = 14
    A-C-E-F = 3+2+3+2 = 10
    ! Critical path A-B-D-F, project time = 14 days
    Float of C and E = 14 - 10 = 4 days
    ## Link to integration testing
    Delay in a module delays its integration
    Stubs and drivers needed, test time squeezed

### Scene 6: 4P = People, Product, Process, Project. Brooks law!
    ## 4P
    People: team skills, motivation
    Product: scope, requirements
    Process: model and activities
    Project: planning, tracking
    ## Brooks law
    Adding people to a late project makes it later
    Communication paths = n(n-1)/2
    n = 5: 10 paths, n = 10: 45 paths

### Scene 7: Change request > Evaluate > Approve > Implement > New baseline
    ## SCM activities
    Identification of configuration items
    Version control
    Change control
    Configuration audit
    Status reporting
    ! Baseline = approved version, changed only by formal change control

### Scene 8: EV: SV = EV - PV, CV = EV - AC, SPI = EV/PV, CPI = EV/AC
    ## Given PV = 100, EV = 80, AC = 90
    SV = 80 - 100 = -20
    CV = 80 - 90 = -10
    SPI = 80 / 100 = 0.8
    CPI = 80 / 90 = 0.89
    ! Behind schedule and over budget
    ## SQA
    Reviews, walkthroughs, inspections, audits, standards

### Scene 9: Known (plan se), Predictable (experience se), Unpredictable
    ## Q: Known vs Predictable
    Known: discovered after careful evaluation of plan, technology, environment
    Predictable: drawn from past project experience
    Unpredictable: cannot be guessed in advance
    ## Q: Components vs Drivers
    Components: performance, cost, support, schedule risk
    Drivers: affect the components, impact levels negligible, marginal, critical, catastrophic
    ! Risk drivers decide how badly a component is hit

### Scene 10: RE = P x L.  Sprints = total points / velocity
    ## Risk exposure
    P = 0.4, loss = 5 lakh
    RE = 0.4 x 5 = 2 lakh
    ! Spend at most about 2 lakh on mitigation
    ## Agile velocity
    Total = 120 points, velocity = 20 per sprint
    ! Sprints = 120 / 20 = 6
    ~ Agile risk: short sprints, review each sprint

### Scene 11: Dilemma: perfect = late + costly, rushed = buggy. Quality != Security
    ## Quality dilemma
    Nobody wants buggy software
    Yet perfect software needs unlimited time and money
    Answer: good enough software, controlled cost
    ## Cost of quality
    Prevention, appraisal, failure (internal + external)
    ## Quality vs security
    ! Not the same: security is one part of quality
    Security = confidentiality, integrity, availability

### Scene 12: McCall: Operation (5) + Revision (3) + Transition (3) = 11
    ## McCall model
    3 perspectives, 11 factors
    Operation: correctness, reliability, usability, integrity, efficiency
    Revision: maintainability, flexibility, testability
    Transition: portability, reusability, interoperability
    ! Mnemonic: CRUIE - MFT - PRI
    ## Relevance
    Each factor has criteria and metrics
    Gives a checklist to measure quality

### Scene 13: CMM: Initial > Repeatable > Defined > Managed > Optimizing
    ## CMM levels
    1 Initial: ad hoc
    2 Repeatable: requirements mgmt, planning, SCM
    3 Defined: organisation wide process, reviews
    4 Managed: quantitative control
    5 Optimizing: defect prevention, innovation
    ! Mnemonic: I Really Do Miss Ordering
    ## ISO 9001
    International standard for quality management systems
    Plan Do Check Act, needs certification audit

### Scene 14: Defect density, failure %, MTBF = MTTF + MTTR
    ## LOC numerical
    12400 LOC = 12.4 KLOC, 310 defects, 36 PM
    Defect density = 310 / 12.4 = 25 per KLOC
    Productivity = 12.4 / 36 = 0.344 KLOC/PM
    ## Failure percentage
    5 failures in 200 runs = 2.5%
    ! Reliability = 97.5%
    ## Reliability
    MTTF 400 h, MTTR 20 h
    MTBF = 420 h
    ! Availability = 400 / 420 = 95.2%

### Scene 15: Maintenance: Corrective, Adaptive, Perfective, Preventive
    ## Maintenance types
    Corrective: fix defects found after delivery
    Adaptive: adapt to OS, hardware, law changes
    Perfective: enhance features and performance
    Preventive: improve maintainability for future
    ! Perfective takes the largest share of effort
    ## Trends
    AI assisted development, DevOps, microservices, cloud native, low code, secure by design
